Necromantle

An original third-person gothic action RPG. Diablo-style rhythm, hordes, and loot, seen from a GTA/RDR-style camera. Working title. Every frame below is a paint-over of the intended composition; HUD, inventory, and loot components are real CSS meant to carry straight into the game.

Camera over-the-shoulder · 6.5–9 m · FOV 60–68°
Class Arcane Sorcerer (one class, made excellent)
Stack Babylon.js 9 · WebGPU → WebGL2 · Vite · TS
Target 60 fps · 1920×1080 · 20–30 enemies
Hover any frame to see camera rules

Enemy silhouette language

Each archetype must be identifiable from shape alone at nine meters. The lineup is to scale against the 1.9 m Sorcerer.

Value ruleEnemies are always darker and less saturated than the player and the ground. Their only color is the rim: rust for common, gold for elite, violet-red for boss.
Elite treatmentGold rim, 1.3× scale, floor aura in the affix's color, nameplate. A pack of ghouls led by an elite Brute must read as a shape before it reads as a count.
E

Ability shape language

Six abilities, six shapes. No two are a glowing sphere. Every one is split into anticipation, travel, impact, and aftermath in the effect library.

ARCANE BOLT

Generator · LMB · +6 energy on hit

Streak. Fast violet lance with a white-cyan head, minor splash. Fired every 0.35 s; slight homing. The sound is a short snap, never a whoosh.

ASTRAL ORB

Spender · RMB · 40 energy

Sphere with a comet tail. Player-head sized, slow, pierces, lights the floor as it passes, detonates at range. Starfall Circlet splits it into three.

FLAME NOVA

Cooldown 10 s · 2 · knockback + burn

Ring. An expanding orange ring with embers and a scorch decal that lingers. Everything inside 4 m is thrown outward. The panic button.

FROST FIELD

Cooldown 14 s · 3 · ground target

Disc with crystals. A pale cyan floor plate with rising shards; edge is a clear rim so the radius is never a guess. Chill, then freeze weaker enemies.

RIFT STEP

Cooldown 6 s · 1 · 8 m · 0.3 s invulnerable

After-image line. Instant. Three fading violet ghosts along a cyan thread. No wind-up, no landing recovery. Boots of the Fold add a charge.

CATACLYSM

Cooldown 60 s · 4 · unlock at 10

Column strikes. A storm of cyan-violet pillars over 6 s with shockwaves and knockback. The room's lighting turns arcane for the duration. Camera pulls back and shakes on each strike.

Screenshot modeHides HUD, freezes AI, locks camera distance and FOV to a preset, and saves a 1920×1080 PNG named by location. Every gap-analysis comparison starts here.
Why it's in the style boardBecause a game only looks like its references if we can measure it against them quickly. The panel is part of the pipeline, not a leftover.
Paint-overs use CSS gradients and SVG silhouettes to lock composition, value structure, color roles and HUD placement. They are not renders. Real geometry will be lit with a cool moon key, warm local lights, fog, bloom below the threshold of white blobs, and no depth of field in play.
Working names (the village Hollowmere, St. Vessel, the Hollow King, Maren, Starfall Circlet) are original placeholders and can change.
