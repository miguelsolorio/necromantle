# Playable characters: plan

The vertical slice ships one class, the Arcane Sorcerer, on purpose (brief §47: one class, made excellent). This plan covers adding more without rewriting what exists, and says when it is cheapest to do.

## What we already have

- Rigged placeholder models with the same skeleton and 75 shared animation clips: Knight, Barbarian, Rogue, Rogue (hooded), Mage (`public/assets/_packs/kaykit-adventurers`, CC0). Weapons and shields come with the pack.
- Data-driven abilities (`content/abilities.ts`) and a runtime that only reads data (`abilities/system.ts`), a cross-fading animator, soft-lock targeting, projectiles, area effects, status effects, pooled VFX and a name-keyed audio layer.
- A storyboard frame for a class-select screen (`docs/storyboard.html`, frame "Choose your class" grammar from the references).

## Candidate roster (original names, distinct combat rhythms)

| Class | Placeholder model | Resource | Rhythm | Abilities (six) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Arcane Sorcerer** (exists) | Mage | Arcane Energy: generated by Bolt, spent on Orb | ranged, crowd control, teleport | Bolt, Orb, Rift Step, Flame Nova, Frost Field, Cataclysm |
| **Sepulcher Knight** | Knight | Fury: builds on every hit taken or dealt, decays out of combat | melee tank, stands in the pack, throws it back | Cleave (generator arc), Shield Rush (charge + knockdown), Iron Ward (absorb shield), Grave Stomp (ring stagger), Bulwark (taunt pull), Judgement (spender: giant slam) |
| **Grave Hunter** | Rogue (hooded) | Focus: fills while standing still or after kills, spent on volleys | ranged physical, traps, mobility | Bolt Shot (generator), Fan of Bolts (spender cone), Vault (backflip dodge), Caltrops (slow field), Mark (target takes more damage), Rain of Bolts (ultimate area) |
| **Pale Reaver** | Barbarian | Blood: gained by kills, drains slowly, unlocks frenzy at full | fast melee, momentum, life steal | Rend (generator), Whirl (spender spin), Leap (gap closer, ground pound), Frenzy (attack speed), Bleed Storm, Harvest (ultimate: heal per kill) |

Each class keeps the same loop: generate → position → spend → clear the pack. The differences are range, resource shape and what "position" means.

## Engineering steps

1. **Class definition** (`content/classes.ts`): `{ id, name, model, weapon, resource: { name, max, regen, gainOnHit, gainOnKill }, abilities: AbilityId[6], passives, stats, animations, accent colour }`. `Player` takes a `ClassDef`; the Sorcerer becomes the first entry with no behaviour change. *Do this during Milestone 5. Half a day.*
2. **Ability registry**: replace the `switch` in `abilities/system.ts` with `abilities/impl/<id>.ts` modules that export `cast(ctx, def)`. Adds melee primitives: cone and arc hitboxes from the player's facing, weapon trails, hit-stop, and lunge magnetism toward the soft target. *Half a day for the registry, one day for the melee primitives.*
3. **Resources**: generalise `energy` into `resource` with the class's rules; the HUD orb takes its colour and name from the class. *Half a day.*
4. **Animation map per class**: clip names for idle, run, strafe, attack variants, cast, hit, death, dodge. The pack shares names across rigs, so this is mostly a table. *Half a day.*
5. **Class select**: an HTML screen before the boot fade with the model on a pedestal (storyboard frame), one save slot per class. *One day.*
6. **Per class content**: six abilities with anticipation/travel/impact/aftermath VFX and sounds, passives, tuning against 8–24 enemy packs. *Two to three days each.*
7. **Items**: class-restricted affixes and legendary powers that change a class's abilities (the loot milestone must know about classes, which is why steps 1–3 should land before it).

## When

- Steps 1–3 now, inside Milestone 5, so loot (Milestone 6) and progression (Milestone 7) are class-aware from the start.
- A second class (the Knight, because melee stresses camera, hitboxes and enemy reactions the most) after Milestone 7, before the world build-out.
- The Hunter and Reaver after the vertical slice loop is fun with two classes. Adding a class then is content, not architecture.

## Risks

- Melee camera: the over-the-shoulder view must keep a surrounded melee character readable; expect a tighter combat distance and stronger hit-stop.
- Animation quality: the pack's attack clips are short and stiff; melee feel leans on trails, hit-stop, camera impulse and enemy reactions more than the swing itself.
- Balance surface: every class multiplies encounter tuning. Keep enemy HP and wave tables shared, tune classes to a common kill-rate target.
